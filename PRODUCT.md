# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS (user's explicit choice; no build step, no framework). No deploy target was specified.

## Users

Primary visitors are prospective clients of a service/agency business who land on the site to evaluate whether to work with them. Their job: quickly understand what the agency offers and decide to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

jordi2 is the marketing/landing site for a service/agency business. It exists to present the agency credibly and convert visitors into inquiries. Success is measured in contact/inquiry actions, not page views.

## Positioning

**Jordi Räumungen** — ein Schweizer Unternehmen für die Räumung von Häusern und Wohnungen. Eigene Positionierungsworte vom Van bzw. der bestehenden Seite: «Räumungen • Umzüge • Weitergehen» und «Wir schaffen Platz. Einfach, zuverlässig und unkompliziert.» Leistungsversprechen laut eigenem Text: Von der kleinen Wohnungsräumung bis zur kompletten Haushaltsauflösung — Räumungen, Transporte, Entsorgung und auf Wunsch Besenreinigung.

## Operating Context

A visitor arrives with minimal prior context, scans the offer and proof, and the defined success action is contacting/inquiring (email, call, or form). No other workflow or integration context has been confirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS only: no framework, no build step. All interactivity (forms, navigation) must work within that constraint.
- Single marketing surface or small multi-page site — page count not yet decided.
- Site language: German (Schweizer Hochdeutsch, ss statt ß).
- Bestehende Navigation als Strukturhinweis: Startseite, Tarife, Wer sind wir?, Kontakt.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over polish: with no supplied evidence yet, the site must earn trust through honest, specific presentation of the real offer once known — never fabricated proof.
2. Contact is the conversion: every design decision should make the inquiry path obvious and low-friction.
3. Clarity first: a first-time visitor must grasp who this is for and what is offered within seconds of landing.
4. Lightweight and durable: static, fast, and maintainable by hand — the stack constraint is a feature, not a limitation.
